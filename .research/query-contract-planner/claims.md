# Claim–evidence ledger

Final audit: 2026-10-14. A proof under a stated model, a numerical calculation,
and an empirical observation are different kinds of evidence. None establishes
novelty by itself.

| ID | Claim | Final status | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| C0 | Generic dependence calculus and the earlier cycle formula alone justify a new paper. | Rejected in prior runs; preserved | [Earlier assessment](../relations-revision/assessment.md), [earlier synthesis](../dependence-calculus/synthesis.md); lineage machinery is established. |
| C1 | Functional constraints improve independent membership-error budgets for existential intersection. | Proved under the independent-source model; established ingredients | [Theory](theory.md): sharp envelope 1-(1-p)^2(1-p²)^(K-2) for K>=2, with explicit K=0,1 cases; exhaustive truth profiles and noise-oracle checks in theory_checks_results.json. Distinct rows and correct source identity are necessary. |
| C2 | The improvement produces a compelling complete application advantage over competent representations. | Not supported in the tested scope | [Results](summary.csv): finite fingerprints save 880 bytes / 0.5104% overall against exact IDs in the manifest, with about 8 times query latency including preparation. Payload-only saving is 37.98%; another deployment could value it differently. Full-domain equality is defeated by ordinary value retrieval. |
| C3 | Constraint-aware allocation is a novel general planning method. | Unsupported; substantial negative novelty evidence | [Primary literature](literature.md): FD-aware probabilistic plans, constrained inference, Monkey, Bloom-collection allocation, retrieval, and filter-aware join planning already cover the ingredients. A new interface does not supply a contribution. |
| C4 | Shared binary-linear fingerprints provide exact finite collision calculations beyond naive independence. | Mathematically valid; established prior art | [Theory](theory.md): rank/characteristic-polynomial calculation is the Crapo–Rota Critical Theorem. Exhaustive finite checks and dependent-triangle counterexample retained. |
| C5 | The sharp product materially improves a competent constraint-aware union allocation. | Refuted for this experiment; a restricted analytic limit is proved | [Theory](theory.md) bounds the extra saving by log2(-log(1-epsilon)/epsilon), or 0.03684214 continuous bits per row at epsilon=.05. [Findings](findings.json): only 6 bytes / 0.00349% overall in the manifest. The analytic limit is for a common-rate logarithmic cost proxy, not every heterogeneous/discrete allocator. |
| C6 | A row-specific finite hash construction gives a sound contract without assuming independence across all witnesses. | Proved under explicit finite-family assumptions; implemented; novelty not established | Degree-at-most-three polynomials with four uniform coefficients over F_(2^61-1); injective tuple encoding; exact modulo collision q; four-wise independence within a witness gives bound 2q+(K-2)q² and FN=0. [Code](code/finite_rows.py), [theory](theory.md), exhaustive F7 constructions. Uniform coefficients and a query fixed independently of construction are essential. |
| C7 | Treating repeated operands or collision events as independent remains sound. | Refuted | Repeated-source example: correct probability 0.04556456 versus naive 0.00000763; same-row and dependent linear-hash counterexamples in [theory](theory.md). Aliases are rejected and repeated atoms canonicalized. |
| C8 | The fixed-C pilot establishes a general advantage for row-specific filters. | Rejected as incomplete comparison | [Preserved pilot](attempts/001-fixed-c/README.md): exact per-row membership-in-C flags plus shared value fingerprints were a missing competent baseline. Final evaluation varies C and stores no per-C cache. |
| C9 | All implementations have the same proved 5% concrete-hash guarantee. | False; guarantees deliberately separated | Finite polynomial theorem is unconditional over uniform coefficients; Bloom occupancy certificates are conditional in an ideal random-function model; binary fuse uses a nominal hash model and has no finite certificate. Seeded pseudorandom experiments do not prove ideal distributions. See [assessment](assessment.md). |
| C10 | Measurements and serialized artifacts reproduce. | Computationally verified for saved cases | [Verifier output](verification.json): all 2304 payloads, exact recorded bounds, and retry counts rebuilt; 5760 representative query outcomes reproduced. [Measured source](measured-source/README.md) retained. Timing values are machine-specific observations. |
| C11 | Zero observed errors or many within-build queries certify the advertised error rate. | Rejected | Construction-level uncertainty avoids treating reused sources as independent samples. Bounded-mean Chernoff/KL intervals are numerically inverted; normal bands are diagnostic. A synthetic constrained-Bloom estimate is 5.615%, with uncertainty spanning 5%. No certified outward rounding is claimed for numerical endpoints. |
| C12 | The constrained-truth theorem or finite-construction alternative warrants an original publication now. | Not established; do not submit | Both nearby alternatives were proved/tested and compared with primary literature. Components are established, and the complete application tradeoff is weak. This is a scoped negative opportunity audit, not a universal impossibility result. [Decision and next gate](assessment.md). |

No claim covers adaptive queries after observing construction information,
arbitrary conditioning/rejection, dynamic updates, partial-row storage, or
security/leakage. Exact constraints describe the underlying database; the noisy
membership relation need not preserve them. Application data are real, while
the query requests are generated and stratified rather than a production trace.

# Confirmed findings

1. Source identity is necessary for repeated frozen-observation semantics. Marginal product rules require additional independence, not merely distinct syntactic operands.
2. Canonical-orbit projection covariance is the product of the three complement factors times the shared-source variance factor; disjoint tuple fibers alone do not imply independence after quotienting. Proof: `../attempts/002-theory/theory.md`, section 2.
3. For monotone lineages and independent one-sided noise, sharp truth-uniform FP/FN contracts are `1-(1-alpha)^s0` and `1-(1-beta)^s1`. Heterogeneous and arbitrary-dependence variants are proved in `../attempts/002-theory/technical-note.tex`. Exact originality is unverified.
4. For normalized binary-witness lineages of source occurrence at most two, standard path/cycle methods give efficient exact fixed-profile probabilities and truth-uniform homogeneous contracts. This is an application of known graph methods.
5. Strict symmetry can preserve marginal rates while inducing known hard counting structure. This is a relational consequence of established results, not a new general hardness theorem.

Evidence was promoted from the literature and theory branches and root prototype branch. See `../claims.md` and `../synthesis.md` for provenance, qualifications, and the decision not to claim standalone publication readiness.

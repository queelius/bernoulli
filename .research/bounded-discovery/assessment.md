## Why the finalist survives

The audited paper is Qin et al., *Privacy-Preserving On-Chain Attestation for
Cross-Domain Data Flows via GBFPlus*, Entropy 28(9):947, published 2026-08-23,
[DOI 10.3390/e28090947](https://doi.org/10.3390/e28090947). The publisher PDF/XML,
Crossref metadata and their hashes are preserved. Section 4.2/Table 1 state
adjacent-domain confidentiality. Sections 5.1–5.2 and Algorithm 4 supply the
public domain registry, unmasked suffix, occupancy, insertion count, full
published table, and timeout finalization without a minimum batch.

For a singleton, the occupied words are exactly all its XOR shares. Their XOR
ends in the adjacent-domain ID, for every hash-support and share realization.
The observer does not need the data identifier, secret prefix, hidden query
positions, or authorization token. Public structural metadata can be identical
for two different domain IDs while the payload views separate them perfectly.
Individual shares are uniform for κ≥2; the joint view is not harmless.

The small extension `occupied cells = successful insertions × κ` certifies
disjoint supports and gives the aggregate XOR of domain IDs. It does not
recover each recipient for a general batch. Authentic count and honest
construction are assumptions; the code does not validate a complete history.

The new contribution is **this concrete counterexample to the published
application's stated confidentiality goal**. XOR sharing, marginal/joint
distinctions, rank-nullity, and secret masking are established. The most direct
generic predecessor is Silva–Kschischang (2011), Lemma 6. Original GBF padding
and OT access (Dong et al. 2013) prevent treating its protocol as the same
exposed-support object. Hao et al. 2024 supplies an existing arbitrary-value
masking baseline; no new masking scheme is claimed.

Exact-title/DOI/GBFPlus searches with correction/comment/critique terms found no
matching prior disclosure as of 2026-10-14. Crossref has no linked correction.
This is a bounded novelty search, not proof that no one has observed it.

## Evidence and review

The manuscript has self-contained proofs. The share-level implementation
reproduces the relevant operation with actual stored arrays, not fresh random
answers. Exhaustive checks cover 256 singleton and 1,024 disjoint-batch cases;
64 separately seeded arrays use the paper's word/domain widths and κ. There
are 19 passing new unit/integration tests and 92 passing existing package
regression tests. New probe coverage is 100% statements and 98.89% branches;
the only missing branch is the driver entry-point alternative. Ruff and strict
mypy pass. No package-core code was changed.

An independent agent checked the source premises, the insertion operation,
finite laws, threat-model scope and publication decision. Its separate finite
enumeration matched all 2,844 successful small insertion configurations and
4,656 full-support failures. This is an independent computational/critical
check, not external peer review. See [review record](review.md).

The actual package's deterministic GF2Retrieval also exhibits the expected
three-query linear relation under three recorded seeds. That conditional
diagnostic grants the stored row and chooses queries after the public seed;
it is retained as background, not advertised as the new attack. The ideal
affine-law experiment checked 8,736 systems and is explicitly attributed to
known theory.

029 The condition requires cκ ≤ m; otherwise it is impossible. Its informational value also depends on

035 Which confidentiality claim is challenged? Consider a publisher with two eligible neighboring
036 domains distinct from it. Fix the public registration table. Internally fix the same publisher, its
037 secret state, data identifier, direction, window, and configuration, and choose either neighboring
038 identifier with equal probability for a singleton insertion. The observer is not given the publisher’s
039 identity or secret state. In source Algorithm 4, the adjacent identifier affects the target suffix but
040 not the derived positions or prefix. Those quantities can therefore be the same in both cases, while
041 the free shares are sampled in each case. Equation (2) identifies the choice with probability one.
042 This comparison concerns one snapshot and its authenticating transaction; digests, signatures, and
043 other ledger records need not be identical between cases.
044     The recovered identifier names the neighbor encoded in that attestation. It does not identify
045 the anonymous publisher, associate the transfer with a particular data identifier, or reconstruct a
046 complete named edge or path. This is also distinct from the source’s Gqpriv game and Theorem 4,
047 which concern distinguishing data identifiers. Separately, source Equation (27) analyzes guessed
048 tuples passing prefix and domain-membership checks under its random-share model. The observer
049 here performs neither validation test: it reads a suffix from a complete share set already identified
050 by occupancy. Those analyses do not establish confidentiality of this suffix.

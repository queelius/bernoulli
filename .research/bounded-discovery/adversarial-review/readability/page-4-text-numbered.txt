030 4    Implications and existing protections
031 The protocol-specific issue is that public occupancy identifies a complete set of shares in an allowed
032 partial batch. The underlying distinction between individual and joint linear observations is
033 established; Silva and Kschischang’s coset-encoding analysis provides a general precedent [2]. The
034 original garbled Bloom filter is a relevant construction contrast: Dong, Chen, and Wen fill unused
035 cells with random words (Algorithm 1, lines 22–26), and their private-set-intersection protocol uses
036 oblivious transfer to supply an intersection structure [3]. That protocol does not give the same
037 unauthorized observer the full server filter together with genuine insertion occupancy.
038     For the all-cell XOR alone, one independent uniform padding word makes the statistic uniform.
039 An observer who retains the genuine occupancy selector can still omit the padding and recover the
040 original occupied-cell XOR. Fresh uniform secret masks on the adjacent identifiers also hide this
041 statistic when the masks are independent of the identifiers and one another. Secret PRF masking of
042 arbitrary values is already used in oblivious key-value retrieval [4]. Neither observation proves a
043 complete repair of GBFPlus. A repair must account for the entire public view, authorized recovery,
044 and key and mask reuse across releases.

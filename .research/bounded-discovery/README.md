# Bounded discovery

Started 2026-10-14 for Alexander Richard Towell.

Screen exactly two new publication hypotheses, in order: limited-verification
deduplication, then joint leakage from frozen retrieval maps. Each gets at most
two decision-changing investigation rounds. Discovery has a roughly one author
day ceiling; a finalist must plausibly need no more than one author week of
remaining work. These are selection limits, not promised completion times.

Earlier research under `../publication-triage/`, `../dependence-calculus/`,
`../relations-revision/`, and `../query-contract-planner/` remains authoritative
within its stated scope. No previous negative finding is silently reopened.

## Plan and gates

1. Deduplication round 1: choose a real operational setting, specify exact state
   and feedback, identify closest primary literature, and test a sharp claim
   against uniform checking and caching.
2. Deduplication round 2, only if useful: test its strongest remaining advantage
   against frequency-aware checking and applicable adaptive-filter methods,
   including authoritative-state and metadata costs.
3. Frozen retrieval round 1: specify observer and construction randomness,
   derive a joint transcript law, and test it on finite exact instances and the
   stored implementation; compare established linear OKVS/privacy guarantees.
4. Frozen retrieval round 2, only if useful: test the strongest remaining
   attack/guarantee/mitigation against standard randomization or masking.
5. Choose at most one finalist. A new interface, ordinary algebraic restatement,
   correctness repair, or additional synthetic sweep does not qualify.
6. If warranted: finish only essential validation and independent review,
   prepare a focused manuscript, verify official venue terms. Otherwise record
   a scoped no-go and stop without adding a third candidate.

New experiment code is an isolated research artifact. Tests must challenge
assumptions and compare against independently computed small cases. Source
changes, if needed, require relevant unit/integration tests and coverage.

\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch15}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
The bare construction of \Cref{sec:bphf-construction}, a minimal perfect
hash with a per-slot fingerprint, is the standard static
approximate-membership structure: \textcite[Sec.~1]{dietzfelbinger-pagh-2008}
describe it, with its overhead of about $\log_2 e$ bits per element, and
remark that its origin is unknown to them. The program's manuscript
\parencite{towell-bernoulli-phf} restates it as a Bernoulli set, lifts it
to maps, and states its relation to the salt-search BHF of chapter~14. The
addressing cost rests on the minimal perfect hashing literature: the
$n \log_2 e$ lower bound of \textcite{fredman-komlos-1984}, the
linear-time scheme of \textcite{fredman-komlos-szemeredi-1984}, and the
compact constructions of \textcite{botelho-pagh-ziviani-2007},
\textcite{belazzougui-2009} (which also gives $k$-perfect hashing),
\textcite{limasset-et-al-2017}, \textcite{esposito-graf-vigna-2020}, and
\textcite{lehmann-sanders-walzer-2024}, with \textcite{lehmann-et-al-2025}
on encoding the seeds that the last two search for. A false-negative
budget spent by dropping members, rather than by sharing slots, is the
lossy dictionary of \textcite{pagh-rodler-2001}. Retrieval-based filters
\parencite{chazelle-et-al-2004,graf-lemire-2020,graf-lemire-2022,dietzfelbinger-et-al-2026-ribbon}
avoid the addressing cost and are the stronger practical comparison on
the FNR-zero arm. Ranked retrieval itself is classical information
retrieval \parencite{manning-raghavan-schutze}; the chapter is agnostic
about the scoring function.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
The FPR-zero reading of the skeleton: the rank table of \Cref{def:bphf},
the observation that it is the perfect hash filter until the query model
is restricted, the witness model in which its false-positive rate is
exactly zero (\Cref{rem:witness-is-query-model}), and the $k$-perfect dial
for a controlled false-negative rate, with the cautions that its misses
are dependent within slots and that occupancy formulas must match the
construction's conditioning. These are presentation and bookkeeping
within the book's framework, not new data structures; the witness model
in particular is an assumption about the application, not a guarantee
the table provides.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Dynamic maintenance of ranks, multi-resolution rank encodings, measured
build and query throughput, and an empirical comparison with the
\emph{lapidary} C++ library (formerly \emph{maph}), the program's
perfect-hash-filter implementation, which realizes the fingerprint
reading but not the rank-table variant.

\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch10}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
Every construction and bound in the chapter is its authors'. Probabilistic
counting from hashed bit patterns is \textcite{flajolet-martin-1985}, and the
streaming model with its space lower bounds is
\textcite{alon-matias-szegedy-1999}. CountMin, its $d \times w$ table of
pairwise-independent rows, and the bound of \Cref{thm:countmin-bound} at $w =
\lceil e/\epsilon \rceil$, $d = \lceil \ln(1/\delta) \rceil$ are
\textcite{cormode-muthukrishnan-2005}. HyperLogLog, the constant $\alpha_m$,
the $1.04/\sqrt{m}$ error of \Cref{prop:hyperloglog-error}, and the limiting
log-normal shape are \textcite{flajolet-et-al-2007}. The MinHash identity
$\Prob{\min h(A) = \min h(B)} = J(A,B)$ and the $k$-hash estimator are
\textcite{broder-1997}, with the hashing requirement made precise by min-wise
independence \parencite{broder-et-al-2000}; locality-sensitive hashing is
\textcite{indyk-motwani-1998}. That merges of sketches with shared hashes equal
sketches of the combined input is the mergeable-summaries property of
\textcite{agarwal-et-al-2013}.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
A common reading of the three sketches as $\Bernoulli{T}$ values whose per-query
error law is a row of a channel matrix (triangular, multiplicative-band,
binomial), and an explicit statement of where that reading stops: rows are
marginals, CountMin's are conditional on the rest of the stream, and queries
against one stored sketch are dependent, so the Kronecker factorization of
chapter~9 does not describe them.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Heavy-hitter, quantile, and inner-product applications; Count-Sketch and other
linear sketches; bias corrections and sparse representations of HyperLogLog;
lower bounds for the three problems; and the generating-function analysis
behind $\alpha_m$ and $1.04$, which is quoted rather than reproduced.

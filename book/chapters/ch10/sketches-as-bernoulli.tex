\section{Sketches as Bernoulli of \texorpdfstring{$T$}{T}}
\label{sec:sketches-as-bernoulli}

A \emph{sketch}\index{sketch} maps a stream or multiset over a universe
$\Universe$ to a compact summary, with a query routine that reconstructs an estimate
$\hat\theta$ of some quantity $\theta$ of the stream from the summary alone.
What makes it a sketch, rather than a compressed representation, is that the
summary grows sublinearly (often poly-logarithmically) in the stream length,
the per-item update is near-constant, and the error $\hat\theta - \theta$ is
bounded by a clean function of the design parameters
(\Cref{sec:bridge-to-sketches}). This chapter builds three, distinguished by
what they estimate, which is to say by their output type.

\emph{CountMin}\index{Count-Min sketch} (\Cref{sec:countmin}) estimates
per-item frequency $f_a$, with $T = \mathbb{N}$ and \emph{one-sided}
error\index{one-sided error}: the construction guarantees
$\hat f_a \ge f_a$ deterministically, the multiset analogue of the Bloom
filter's one-sided membership report (\Cref{ch:approximate-sets}).
\emph{HyperLogLog}\index{HyperLogLog} (\Cref{sec:hyperloglog}) estimates distinct-item cardinality
$n$, again with $T = \mathbb{N}$ but with two-sided relative error of order
$1/\sqrt{m}$ in the register count $m$. \emph{MinHash}\index{MinHash} (\Cref{sec:minhash-lsh})
estimates Jaccard similarity\index{Jaccard similarity} $J(A,B)$, with $T = [0,1]$ and symmetric error of
variance $J(1-J)/k$ in the signature length $k$.

The chapter-9 vocabulary gives all three the same shape. Each is a function
$s : \Universe^* \to T$ whose per-query error can be summarized by a
row-stochastic channel $Q$ (\Cref{def:n-ary-channel}) with rows indexed by the
true value $\theta$ and columns by the report $\hat\theta$: CountMin's $Q$ is
upper triangular (one-sided), HyperLogLog's concentrates in a multiplicative
band (relative error), MinHash's is binomial along each row. This is a reading
of known constructions, not a new analysis of them, and it has a definite scope.
A row is a per-query marginal, and for CountMin it is conditional on the rest of
the stream, since the collision mass depends on the other items' frequencies.
Several queries against one stored sketch share its hash functions and counters,
so their errors are dependent: Axiom~1 (\Cref{ax:element-indep}) fails across
them, and the Kronecker factorization of \Cref{thm:n-ary-kronecker} does not give
their joint law. Each of the next three sections follows one template:
construction, error bound, $\Bernoulli{T}$ reading, merge.
\Cref{sec:ch10-bridge} records what the three merge rules have in common.

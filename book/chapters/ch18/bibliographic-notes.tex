\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch18}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
Searchable encryption begins with \textcite{song-wagner-perrig-2000};
\textcite{curtmola-et-al-2006} gave the adaptive security definitions and
the inverted-index constructions that made exact search efficient, with
an explicit leakage profile (which items match, whether queries repeat).
The Bloom-filter per-document index with keyed probes is the secure index
of \textcite{goh-2003}. How much the standard leakage can be suppressed is
studied by \textcite{kamara-moataz-ohrimenko-2018}, and how much an
informed attacker extracts from it by \textcite{cash-et-al-2015}. Bloom
filters also encode identifiers for privacy-preserving record linkage
\parencite{schnell-bachteler-reiher-2009}, where cryptanalysis recovers
plaintext from filter patterns \parencite{kuzu-et-al-2011}, a reminder
that filter bits leak through frequency even when keyed. Measuring leakage
as information in a posterior belongs to quantitative information flow
\parencite{smith-2009}. The confidentiality analysis of Bloom-filter
encrypted search was the subject of the author's master's thesis
\parencite{towell-encrypted-search-thesis}; the bandit estimator and the
moving-window bootstrap of \Cref{sec:adversarial-estimation} are from
\textcite{towell-es-conf-mab}; the entropy-ratio manuscript
\parencite{towell-maximizing-confidentiality} develops information-based
confidentiality measures further.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
The encrypted-search problem read through the error model: the per-document
and inverted-index layouts as cipher maps with profile $(\fprate, 0)$, the
three-region conjunction rate, the false-positive cover as the chapter 7
posterior under a stated observation model, and
\Cref{prop:multi-query-leakage}, which separates leakage that adds across
queries from the geometric loss of cover across independently keyed
instances. These are elementary consequences of known tools, assembled
with their limits stated.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Formal SSE security definitions and proofs, dynamic and forward-private
schemes, leakage-abuse and volume attacks beyond their citation, ranked
and multi-keyword SSE, and any treatment of malicious servers.

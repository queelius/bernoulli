\section*{Bibliographic Notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Bibliographic Notes}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch9}

\paragraph{The approximate-maps manuscript.}
The lift of the noisy bit $\Bernoulli{\BoolType}$ to the type-general
$\Bernoulli{T}$, with the n-ary channel matrix $Q$ organizing its per-block
output distributions, follows \textcite{towell-bernoulli-maps}: the
$T$-valued framing of \Cref{sec:atom-to-function}, the $K \times L$ channel of
\Cref{def:n-ary-channel}, the Kronecker factorization
\Cref{thm:n-ary-kronecker} with its parameter count
\Cref{cor:n-ary-parameter-count}, and the capacity formulation of
\Cref{sec:n-ary-capacity} are from it. This chapter recalls \Cref{sec:kronecker}
and \Cref{sec:bsc-capacity} as the model-order-two special cases the n-ary
results generalize, and exhibits the 3-class classifier and the counter as
concrete non-Boolean instances.

\paragraph{N-ary channels and Blahut--Arimoto.}
The discrete memoryless channel is standard, with \textcite{cover-thomas} the
depth reference: their chapter~7 defines capacity as the supremum of mutual
information over input distributions and treats the symmetric and weakly
symmetric special cases whose closed forms \Cref{sec:n-ary-capacity} uses, and
their chapter~10 develops the Blahut--Arimoto algorithm, the coordinate ascent
on the mutual information that makes the general n-ary capacity of
\Cref{prop:n-ary-capacity} computable rather than merely defined.

\paragraph{Shannon's framework.}
The channel-capacity vocabulary originates in \textcite{shannon-1948}: the
entropy of \Cref{sec:entropy-refresher}, the capacity $C = \max_{P_X} I(X; Y)$
of \Cref{sec:n-ary-capacity}, and the source-coding theorem connecting them.
Shannon's channel as a probabilistic map from input to output alphabet under a
fixed transition kernel is exactly the framing of \Cref{def:n-ary-channel}: $Q$
is his transition matrix, $K$ and $L$ his alphabet sizes, the chapter's capacity
his capacity. The sketch literature previewed in \Cref{sec:bridge-to-sketches}
is taken up with its citations in \Cref{ch:sketches-estimators}, where the named
constructions belong, rather than with the abstract framework that subsumes
them.

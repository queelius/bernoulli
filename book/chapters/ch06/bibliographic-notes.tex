\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch6}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
The rate formulas of \Cref{sec:union-of-sets,sec:other-set-ops} are the gate
rules of chapter~3 applied pointwise to membership, and that computation is
old. Forward propagation of signal probabilities\index{signal probability}
through AND, OR, and NOT gates with independent inputs is due to
\textcite{parker-mccluskey-1975}, and probabilistic transfer
matrices\index{probabilistic transfer matrix}
\parencite{krishnaswamy-et-al-2008} carry the
same calculus, asymmetric rates included, through whole circuits. In
databases the same rules are the independent-or and independent-and of
probabilistic relational algebra\index{probabilistic relational algebra}
\parencite{fuhr-roelleke-1997} and of query evaluation on probabilistic
databases\index{probabilistic database} \parencite{dalvi-suciu-2007}. Bitwise
union and intersection of Bloom filters are folklore surveyed by
\textcite{broder-mitzenmacher-2004}, and \textcite{papapetrou-siberski-nejdl-2010}
estimate the sizes of unions and intersections from filters. The
region-weighted forms used here follow the composition manuscript
\textcite{towell-bernoulli-composition}.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
Two things, both modest. First, the perfect-filter-line classification of
\Cref{sec:cancellation,sec:perfect-filter-line}: each one-sided axis is
closed under union and intersection, complement exchanges the axes, and
same-axis difference leaves the line. These facts are elementary once the
region rates are written down; their use is organizational, for chapters~14
and~15. Second, the explicit caveat that a composed set is higher order, so
its summary pair is not a valid operand for further composition
(\Cref{rem:composing-composed}); exact propagation is block by block, and the
honest two-number summary is a worst-case bound.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Symmetric difference and the propagation of uncertain rates as intervals,
both treated in the composition manuscript; operands that share hash
functions or storage, beyond \Cref{rem:cross-set-independence}, which
chapter~16 takes up as correlated noise; and estimation of the cardinality of
a composed set from its operands.

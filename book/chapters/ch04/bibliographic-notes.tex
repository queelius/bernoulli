\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch4}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
The Kronecker product and its algebra (mixed products, vectorization) are
standard \parencite[Ch.~4]{horn-johnson-matrix}. That independent components
form a product channel, whose transition matrix is the tensor product of the
per-component matrices, is the memoryless (extension) channel of information
theory \parencite[Ch.~7]{cover-thomas}, and it is the tensor-product noise model
of quantum readout-error mitigation, where per-qubit $2 \times 2$ response
matrices are combined by Kronecker products \parencite{bravyi-et-al-2021,smith-et-al-2021}.
The Jensen gap for random rates is the positive correlation of a mixture of
independent trials. The two-axiom formulation, and the counting that calls the
collapse from an exponential to a linear number of parameters ``parametric
parsimony,'' come from the source manuscripts
\parencite{towell-bernoulli-sets,towell-bernoulli-data-type}.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
Presentation and scope. The axioms are separated and stated conditionally on
the realized rates, the conditional and unconditional forms of the
factorization are distinguished (\Cref{thm:kronecker},
\Cref{cor:kronecker-unconditional}), and the distinction is tied to the
structures that need it: a Bloom filter satisfies Axiom~1 only given its array.
The formal type is defined so that its scope is visible: NOT is an operation on
it, while AND and OR over distinct latent bits leave it
(\Cref{prop:boolean-closure}, \Cref{sec:boolean-formulas}).

\paragraph{Left out.}
Dependent errors are not modeled here; Markov-correlated channels are mentioned
only to show what Axiom~2 excludes, and chapter~16 treats correlated and
adaptive errors. Identifiability and estimation of the $2m$ parameters from
data are not developed beyond the single-query estimators of chapter~1.

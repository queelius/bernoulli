\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch19}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
Everything in this chapter is standard linear algebra and probability.
Kronecker products, the mixed-product rule, and their spectra are in
\textcite{horn-johnson-matrix}; stochastic matrices and their products are
the algebra of finite Markov chains \parencite{levin-peres-wilmer-2017,mitzenmacher-upfal},
whose sources differ, as this book's chapters do, on whether matrices act
on row or column vectors. The noisy-parity formula of
\Cref{prop:parity-spectral} is Lemma~1 of \textcite{gallager-1962} and
Matsui's piling-up lemma \parencite{matsui-1993}; its $\pm 1$-bias and
Walsh readings belong to Fourier analysis on the Boolean cube
\parencite{odonnell-2014}, and bias accounting through noisy gates to the
tradition of \textcite{von-neumann-1956}, \textcite{pippenger-1988}, and
\textcite{evans-schulman-1999}. The matrix calculus of error through
general logic circuits is the probabilistic transfer matrix of
\textcite{krishnaswamy-et-al-2008}, preceded by the signal-probability
method of \textcite{parker-mccluskey-1975}. The density-matrix and POVM
facts are textbook \parencite{nielsen-chuang-2010}, and the readout-error
literature already models computational-basis measurement by
tensor-product and correlated assignment matrices, with their inversion
and calibration
\parencite{bravyi-et-al-2021,nation-et-al-2021,smith-et-al-2021,maciejewski-zimboras-oszmaniec-2020,maciejewski-et-al-2021,hamilton-et-al-2020};
the program's companion manuscript on readout
\parencite{towell-bernoulli-quantum-readout} applies known tools there and
needs the per-latent-assignment correction of
\Cref{prop:latent-propagation} for Boolean post-processing.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
A restatement, not new content: the book's set-theoretic results are
written as operator identities (serial composition as a product, Axiom~1
as a factorization, parsimony as additivity of factor parameters, parity
as a product of eigenvalues), with the transpose relation to the
row-stochastic convention of chapters~2 and~9 made explicit. It also
fixes two scope statements of earlier drafts: the classical model is exact
for diagonal measurements of any state, not only of diagonal states, and
the spectral calculus does not make Boolean outputs two-rate bits.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Readout-error mitigation and its statistics, crosstalk models beyond
their citation, non-diagonal measurements, and any quantum algorithmic
content beyond the amplitude-amplification contrast of
\Cref{rem:quantum-amplification}.

\section{Bridge}
\label{sec:ch12-bridge}

The chapter traced one object from three sides.
\Cref{sec:sets-to-relations} read an approximate relation as the chapter-5
framework on the pair universe $T \times U$ (\Cref{ch:approximate-sets}), with
the chapter-9 binary-function view (\Cref{ch:approximate-functions}) an
equivalent reading. \Cref{sec:relation-composition} added the relational
operators: by \Cref{thm:join-rates}, the natural join of two FNR-zero relations
stays FNR-zero with a false-positive rate that is the chapter-6 intersection
rate on the triple universe (\Cref{ch:set-composition}), the bare product
$\fprate_1 \fprate_2$ only in the sparse regime where out-of-join triples are
dominated by the both-unrelated region. \Cref{sec:approximate-joins}
instantiated the machinery on the MinHash-LSH similarity join
(\Cref{sec:minhash-lsh}), reading its FPR/FNR as the chapter-12 join rates
specialized to a sketch-based predicate.

\Cref{ch:algebra-types} closes Part~III with the type algebra that unifies
approximate sets, functions, relations, and sketches into one algebra of
$\Bernoulli{T}$ constructors. An approximate relation is $\Bernoulli{\BoolType}$
on a product universe, equivalently $\Bernoulli{T \times U}$ in the
type-constructor view, and the composition rules each prior chapter derived by
direct calculation become functorial properties of the product type. Chapter~12
is one of the first instances of that unification: an approximate relation reread
as $\Bernoulli{T \times U}$ inherits its composition rules from the algebraic
structure of the product type rather than from a separate derivation.

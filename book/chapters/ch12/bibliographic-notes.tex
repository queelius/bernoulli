\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch12}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
The relational model and its operators are due to \textcite{codd-1970}. A
probabilistic relational algebra\index{probabilistic relational algebra}, whose
tuples carry event expressions evaluated under independence of basic events, goes
back to \textcite{fuhr-roelleke-1997}; tuple-independent query evaluation
\parencite{dalvi-suciu-2007}, its dichotomy \parencite{dalvi-suciu-2012}, and the
survey of \textcite{suciu-2011} followed, with Trio and ULDB
\parencite{widom-2005-trio, benjelloun-et-al-2008} and MayBMS
\parencite{huang-2009-maybms} as systems. Boolean lineage and provenance
semirings\index{provenance semiring} \parencite{green-karvounarakis-tannen-2007}
already record how tuples contribute to an answer; once a truth profile and a
construction law are fixed, every query probability in this chapter is an instance
of probabilistic\index{probabilistic database} inference over such lineage, and the
fact that the uncertainty originates in a compact representation rather than in the
data does not evade that prior art. Conditioning probabilistic databases
\parencite{koch-olteanu-2008} and dissociation bounds for repeated sources
\parencite{gatterbauer-suciu-2014} are the established tools for the two
situations \Cref{sec:query-contracts} touches. The contracts themselves are
elementary consequences of monotone certificates (sensitivity equals certificate
complexity for monotone functions, \textcite{nisan-1991}; sensitivity-based noise
bounds, \textcite{gopalan-et-al-2016}). Constraint repair is the subject of
consistent query answering \parencite{bertossi-2011}. Approximating a relation with
a Bloom filter \parencite{bloomfilter} in query processing goes back at least to
Bloom-filter semijoins \parencite{mullin-1990}, and the similarity join combines
MinHash \parencite{broder-1997} with locality-sensitive hashing
\parencite{indyk-motwani-1998}, systematized as a set-similarity operator by
\textcite{sarawagi-kirpal-2004}.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
The chapter is a re-synchronized presentation of \textcite{towell-bernoulli-relations},
revised in October 2026 after an audit retracted several earlier claims. It contributes
explicit source semantics in the book's notation, audited operator and invariant
formulas, the separation of marginal rates from shared-source dependence, and a
reproducible calibration study whose finding (tuple-sized sources at $0.05$ give a
boundary-profile query error near $0.33$; calibrated sizing restores about $0.05$)
is a design lesson rather than a new theorem. A targeted search did not locate the
identical query-calibration formulation, which does not establish originality.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Negation, aggregates, adaptive query selection, approximate attribute comparisons,
weighted model counting beyond the path and cycle class, construction-specific
guarantees for concrete hash families, and any confidentiality claim (see the scope remark in \Cref{sec:ch12-bridge}).

\section{Notes and Lineage}
\label{sec:bib-notes-ch17}

\paragraph{Lineage.}
The properties asked of a cipher map are older than the name. Many
encodings per plaintext to flatten frequencies is homophonic substitution
\parencite{jendal-kuhn-massey-1989}; plausible decodings under wrong keys
are the distribution-transforming encoders of honey encryption
\parencite{juels-ristenpart-2014}; key-hiding map storage whose non-keys
decode to random-looking values is the oblivious key-value store
\parencite{garimella-et-al-2021,bienstock-et-al-2023}; computation through
keyed lookup tables is white-box cryptography \parencite{chow-et-al-2002};
equality-revealing hashes are point-function obfuscation
\parencite{canetti-1997,lynn-prabhakaran-sahai-2004}; and encryption that
keeps a chosen property testable is property-preserving encryption
\parencite{pandey-rouselakis-2012}. The keyed Bloom filter as an encrypted
membership structure is the secure index of \textcite{goh-2003}, and the
plausible deniability its false positives provide is part of that design.
Leakage beyond the result bit is the subject of the searchable-encryption
literature \parencite{curtmola-et-al-2006,cash-et-al-2015,kamara-moataz-ohrimenko-2018};
adaptive attacks on filters are formalized by \textcite{naor-yogev-2015},
\textcite{clayton-patton-shrimpton-2019}, and \textcite{filic-et-al-2022}.
FHE is due to \textcite{gentry-2009}; \textcite{goldreich-1996} is the
foundational reference for computation under cryptographic constraints.
The program's own treatments are the cipher-map manuscript
\parencite{towell-cipher-maps}, a note on Boolean algebra over trapdoor
sets \parencite{towell-boolean-trapdoor}, and the entropy-ratio manuscript
on quantitative confidentiality \parencite{towell-maximizing-confidentiality}.

\paragraph{What this chapter adds.}
A reading, not new cryptography: the error profile of a keyed approximate
structure is a Bernoulli atom, so the per-query algebra of Parts I to IV
(serial products, the three-region set rates, the chapter 7 posterior)
applies to it unchanged. Two limits are stated that earlier drafts
blurred: serial composition is a per-query law that fails across queries
sharing an intermediate value, and the false-positive cover is one leakage
channel of one structure, eroding geometrically across independently keyed
instances.

\paragraph{Left out.}
Formal security definitions and proofs, key management, side-channel
defenses, malicious servers, and the leakage-abuse and inference attacks
that matter most in deployed encrypted search.

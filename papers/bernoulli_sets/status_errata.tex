\textbf{Status and errata (October 2026).}
This is the final version of this preprint; no further revisions are planned.
It is superseded by Chapters~2, 4, 5, and~7 (\emph{Binary Channels and Model
Order}; \emph{Two Axioms and Kronecker Factorization}; \emph{Approximate Sets
and Predicates}; \emph{Classification Measures}) of the monograph
\emph{Bernoulli Types: Two-Sided Error in Approximate Membership, Retrieval,
and Computation} (A.~Towell, 2026),
\url{https://metafunctor.com/latex/bernoulli-monograph/book.html}
(project hub: \url{https://metafunctor.com/research/bernoulli/}).
Please cite the monograph in preference to this preprint.

\textbf{Corrections in this version.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
\item Abstract, \cref{sec:higher_order_model,sec:conclusion}: set-theoretic
  compositions are higher-order models; removed the claim that they inherit the two-rate theory.
\item \Cref{sec:higher_order_model}: fixed the approximate union operator (rate
  arguments swapped; positives are lost with probability $\fnrate$, not $\tprate$).
\item \Cref{sec:bernoulli_model}: a Bloom filter meets \cref{asm:element_indep} only
  conditionally on its bit array (two-cell example: $5/8$ versus $9/16$).
\item \Cref{sec:asymtotic}: withdrew the maximum-entropy and worst-case-interval
  claims; correlated errors widen the intervals.
\item \Cref{sec:asymtotic}: the intervals are probability intervals for the
  realized rates, not confidence intervals for unknown parameters.
\item Abstract, \cref{sec:bernoulli_model,sec:conclusion}, introduction: removed novelty claims.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Predecessors credited.} Per element, the model is an asymmetric binary
channel~\cite{shannonBSC}, Bross's misclassification
model~\cite{bross1954misclassification}, and Warner's randomized
response~\cite{warner1965}. Probabilistic sets are due to
Hirota~\cite{hirota1981probabilistic}; two-sided-error filters include
retouched~\cite{donnet2006retouched} and generalized~\cite{laufer2011generalized}
Bloom filters; Bloom-filter set operations appear in~\cite{broderMitzenmacher}.
Binomial counts, normal limits, and channel cascades are textbook
material~\cite{feller2,coverThomas}; no novelty is claimed.
A dated list of changes is in \texttt{ERRATA.md} in the source repository.

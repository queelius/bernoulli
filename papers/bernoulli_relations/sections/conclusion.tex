\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We modeled the approximate relation as a Bernoulli random approximate set over
the tuple universe and traced one question through the relational algebra: what
happens to error that a representation injects at the tuple level when the
relation is consumed at the level of operators and invariants?

Three clusters of results answer it. Through the \emph{operators}, error
propagates with sharply different signatures: selection is rate-transparent,
projection amplifies the false-positive rate by its fan-in while preserving
element-wise independence, and a join breaks element-wise independence, its
output tuples correlated through shared components, in possible-worlds terms the
shared-lineage dependence of probabilistic databases, here carried as a
closed-form factor. The \emph{invariants} are uniformly fragile: reflexivity,
symmetry, single-valuedness, and transitivity each survive approximation only
with probability tending to zero in the universe, at per-invariant rates we give
exactly. And the \emph{closure dichotomy} sorts the repairs. A class-induced
invariant is free: one canonical trial per orbit preserves it deterministically
at base rates and base space (\cref{thm:canonical_test}), and an optional
$\log_2 m$-bit surcharge buys an $m$-trial recall boost
(\cref{cor:invariant_space_cost}). Transitivity, which no equivalence induces
(\cref{lem:no_quotient}), cascades to depth $\lceil \log_2 k \rceil$ and admits
no bounded repair (\cref{thm:transitivity_cascade}). The cut is decomposability,
not arity: that is the paper's takeaway.

The dichotomy should be read beside, not inside, the database-repair and
consistent-query-answering tradition~\cite{bertossi-2011-repairing}. There, an
exact database violates an integrity constraint and the question is the
\emph{computational complexity} of repairing or querying it. Here, the relation
is a compressed approximate representation of an exact, consistent base, and the
question is the \emph{rate and space price} of restoring an invariant that the
representation itself broke. The axes are independent: a constraint class can be
cheap on one and expensive on the other.

Three directions stay open. The deletion repair of single-valuedness is priced
under uniform tie-breaking; whether a value-aware tie-breaker does better, and
what the matching converse is, we did not pursue. The trials here are
independent (\cref{asm:ber_trial}). A correlated-trial model would need the
dichotomy re-examined, and a join output read back as the base relation for
further algebra is exactly such a model. And the trapdoor application of
\cref{sec:trapdoor_rel_model} invites an empirical study at database scale,
where the constants behind these closed forms meet real workloads.

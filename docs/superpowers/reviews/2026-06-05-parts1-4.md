## HIGH (fixed)

**H1 (quality): ch3 §3.4 Miller-Rabin design rule was wrong by a factor of 4.**
`miller-rabin.tex:113-114` stated "choose $k \ge -2\log_2\epsilon$." Solving $(1/4)^k \le \epsilon$ gives $k \ge -\tfrac12\log_2\epsilon = \log_4(1/\epsilon)$. The stated rule is 4x too large and contradicted both the section's own example ($k=5$ reaches $10^{-3}$, $k=20$ reaches $10^{-12}$; the wrong rule would demand $k\approx80$ for $10^{-12}$) and the correct restatement in §11.4 (`sample-complexity.tex:50`). **Fixed** to $k \ge \tfrac12\log_2(1/\epsilon) = \log_4(1/\epsilon)$, with the example values cited inline to lock consistency.

**H2 (math): ch7 §7.5 "30% more bits" should be about 16%.**
`bloom-diagnostics.tex:77` claimed re-tuning the Bloom filter from FPR 0.0102 to 0.005 at fixed $k$ "costs about 30% more bits." Exact inversion of $(1-e^{-kn/m})^k$ at fixed $k=7$ gives 15.7% (optimal-$k$ regime 15.5%; from the design rate 0.0082, 10.6%). No interpretation yields 30%. **Fixed** to about 16%. Every other §7.5 number (Wilson interval, PPV 0.9158 and its CI, Route B $\pi\approx0.163$) verified exact.

## MEDIUM (fixed)

**M1 (quality): ch3 §3.2 `prop:boolean-closure` overclaimed "polynomials in the input rates."**
The AND/OR false-positive rate is prior-dependent (a prevalence-weighted rational expression, the same three-region structure as ch6 intersection), not a polynomial in the input rates alone, as the section's own §3.1 duality discussion notes two paragraphs earlier. **Fixed** the proposition to "prior-free rate is a polynomial in the input rates and prior-dependent rate is a rational function of the input rates and the prevalences."

**M2 (math): ch7 §7.3 coverage table figures were optimistic.**
`confidence-intervals.tex` table and body line 36 reported, at $n_0=100, p=0.05$: Wald $\approx0.92$, Wilson $\approx0.94$, Jeffreys $\approx0.945$. Exact binomial enumeration gives Wald 0.878, Wilson 0.966, Jeffreys 0.935, CP 0.983. The Wald 0.92 was optimistic, Wilson 0.94 understated. **Fixed** the row to the exact single-point coverages (Wald 0.88, Wilson 0.97, CP $\ge0.95$, Jeffreys 0.94), reworded the caption to state they are exact single-point coverages oscillating along a sawtooth in $n_0$, and corrected the body "near 0.92" to "near 0.88."
